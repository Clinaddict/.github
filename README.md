## About Us

Clinaddict is a pioneering addiction treatment center located in Cannes, France, specializing in innovative laser therapy and holistic wellness programs. We help individuals overcome addictions and optimize their lifestyle through evidence-based, non-invasive treatments.

### Our Specialties

🚭 **Smoking Cessation**  
Laser auriculotherapy for nicotine addiction

🌿 **Cannabis Withdrawal**  
Gentle, effective laser-assisted therapy

⚖️ **Weight Management**  
Laser therapy combined with lifestyle coaching

🧘 **Stress & Burnout**  
Holistic wellness programs

😴 **Sleep Disorders**  
Insomnia treatment and sleep optimization

🎯 **Comprehensive Wellness**  
Custom programs for menopause, tinnitus, and sugar cravings
